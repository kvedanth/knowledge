    Architecture Trade-off Answer Method (TRADED Framework)

    Use this framework when answering any trade-off or design question in an interview:

    • Trade-off — name the competing dimensions (cost vs latency, accuracy vs speed, build vs buy)
    • Requirements — anchor to stated NFRs and business context
    • Alternatives — present 2–3 options with pros and cons
    • Decision — state your recommended choice
    • Evidence — cite a benchmark, case study, or first-principles reasoning
    • Dependencies — flag prerequisites, risks, and reversibility
